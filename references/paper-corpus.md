# Expanded Representative Paper Corpus Database (20 Papers)

This database expands the corpus from 12 to 20 landmark research papers from Prof. Jianhua Qin's laboratory (Dalian Institute of Chemical Physics, CAS; and School of Biomedical Engineering, USTC).
